02/Architecture

Your data never leaves your machine.

Every Forge app follows one blueprint: local processing, no cloud, nothing to breach.

  1. 01

    Runs on your device

    Installed locally. No account, no sign-in.

  2. 02

    Processed locally

    The work happens on your CPU, not on someone else's server.

  3. 03

    Secured at rest

    Data stays on your disk. Secrets, like VaultForge entries, are AES-256 encrypted.

  4. 04

    Zero cloud dependency

    No servers to go down, get breached or lock you out.

Privacy by design

No server holds your data, so there's nothing to read, leak or hand over.

Built for performance

Small, focused desktop apps that start fast and stay light on resources.

What leaves your machine

  • Your files & dataNever
  • Passwords & keysNever
  • Account requiredNone
