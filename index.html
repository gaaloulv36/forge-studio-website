02/Custom work

Your version of the tool.

Off-the-shelf software makes you fit around it. A custom build starts from a working product and changes what matters to you.

01

Tell me what you need

Send a short message: which product, what's missing, who will use it. We'll go through the details together.

02

I adapt the product

I build your changes on top of the working app and show you progress along the way, so there are no surprises at the end.

03

You get your own build

You receive a version made for your team, ready to install, with your name on it rather than mine.

What a custom version can include

Examples. Ask if you have something else in mind.

Your branding

Your company name, logo and colours throughout the app and its installer.

Extra checks

Rules for your own environment: the programs you allow, the folders you watch, the limits you set.

Reports

Exports and summaries in the format your team or your auditors already use.

Integrations

Connect the app to the tools you already run, such as your ticketing system, chat or internal services.
