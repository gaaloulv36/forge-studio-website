02/Security

How your data is protected.

All cryptography happens on your machine. What's saved to disk is ciphertext, and the only key that opens it is your master password.

AES-256 encryption

Every entry is encrypted with AES-256 in memory, before anything is written. [MODE, e.g. AES-256-GCM]

Master password

Never stored and never sent. The encryption key is derived from it on your device. [KEY DERIVATION, e.g. Argon2id / PBKDF2 and parameters]

Where data lives

Your vault is a local, encrypted file on your machine. No account and no cloud copy. [BACKUP / EXPORT OPTIONS]
